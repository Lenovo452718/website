Sizing & Fit 5 questions
Use the Size Guide available on each product page. Measure your waist and hips in inches, then match to the size chart. When in doubt, size up for a more relaxed fit.
Most of our styles run true to size. Our high-rise and skinny styles have a slight stretch due to the elastane blend. For baggy and wide-leg styles, you can size down for a relaxed look.
Minimal shrinkage may occur after the first wash. We recommend washing at 30°C and air drying rather than tumble drying to preserve the shape and color of your jeans.
Our standard inseam is approximately 30–32 inches depending on the style. Wide-leg and flare styles tend to have a longer inseam to complement the silhouette. Exact measurements are on each product page.
For fitted styles (high-rise, skinny), go with the smaller size — the fabric has stretch. For relaxed styles (baggy, wide-leg), choose the larger size for comfort. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you decide.
