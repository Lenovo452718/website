Policies

Shipping & Exchanges

Everything you need to know about delivery and size exchanges — clear, simple, and honest.

⚠️

No Returns or Refunds

All sales are final. Once your order is delivered, we do not accept returns or issue refunds. Please review your size carefully before ordering. We do however offer size exchanges within 3 business days of receiving your order.

🚚 Shipping

Standard Delivery

Free

On all orders across Morocco. Delivered within 2–5 business days.

Express Delivery

30 MAD

Priority dispatch. Delivered within 1–2 business days.

Order Processing

24h

Orders placed before 3 PM are processed the same day.

  • ✓ You will receive a confirmation message once your order is dispatched.
  • ✓ Delivery is available across all regions of Morocco.
  • ✓ Someone must be present to receive the package upon delivery.

🔄 Size Exchange Policy

  • ✓ You may request a size exchange within 3 business days of receiving your order.
  • ✓ The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags still attached.
  • ✓ Exchanges are for size only — same product, different size.
  • ✗ We do not accept returns for refund once the item has been delivered.
  • ✗ Worn, washed, or damaged items are not eligible for exchange.
  • ✗ Requests made after 3 business days will not be accepted.

Exchange Fee

35 MAD

A flat fee of 35 MAD is charged for every size exchange to cover re-shipping costs. This must be paid before the new size is dispatched.

📋 How to Request an Exchange

1

Contact us within 3 business days

Send us a message via WhatsApp or the contact form with your order number and the size you need.

2

Confirm item condition

Send a photo of the item — unworn, with tags still attached — so we can verify eligibility.

3

Pay the 35 MAD exchange fee

Once confirmed, pay the 35 MAD re-shipping fee. Your new size will be dispatched immediately after.

4

Return the original item

Hand the original item to our delivery agent when they bring your new size. No extra hassle.

Have a question?

Our team is here to help. Reach us on WhatsApp or through the contact page and we'll get back to you as quickly as possible.
