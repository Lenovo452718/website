⚠ Important — Please Read Before Ordering
🚫

No Returns. No Refunds.

All sales are final. Once your order has been delivered, we do not accept returns or issue refunds under any circumstances. Please check your size carefully using our size guide before placing your order.

The only exception is a size exchange within 3 business days of delivery — see the policy below for full details.

PolicySize Exchange

✓  What you CAN do

Request a size exchange within 3 business days of receiving your order.

Exchange for the same product in a different size.

Return the item to our delivery agent when your new size arrives.

✗  What you CANNOT do

Request a refund or return — all sales are final once delivered.

Exchange a worn, washed, or damaged item.

Make a request after 3 business days of delivery.

  • ✓ Item must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags still attached.
  • ✗ Exchanges are for size only — you cannot swap to a different product or color.

Exchange Fee

35

MAD

Re-shipping fee applies

A flat fee of 35 MAD is required for every size exchange to cover re-shipping costs. This must be paid before the new size is dispatched to you.
