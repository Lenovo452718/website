Size
Guide

All our jeans are cut in Moroccan sizing. Use the measurements below to find your perfect fit. When between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed feel.

How to measure

01

Waist

61 – 89 cm 24" – 35"

The narrowest part of your torso, just above the belly button. Wrap the tape snugly — breathe normally and don't hold your breath.

02

Hip

86 – 114 cm 34" – 45"

The fullest part of your hips and seat, approximately 20 cm below the waist. Stand feet together and keep the tape level all the way around.

03

Thigh

Upper thigh Upper thigh

Measure around the fullest part of the thigh, just below the hip joint. Useful for wide-leg and baggy fits.

Sizing
Tips
  • Always measure yourself — don't rely on clothing from other brands as sizes vary.
  • Our denim has minimal stretch, so measure carefully and don't round down.
  • If your waist and hip measurements fall in different sizes, size up to accommodate the hips.
  • Still unsure? Contact us on WhatsApp — we'll help you pick the right size.
