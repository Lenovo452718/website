About Our Sizing

All our jeans are cut in Moroccan sizing. Use the measurements below to find your perfect fit. When between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed feel.

How to Measure

Use a soft measuring tape
Waist 61 – 89 cm

The narrowest part of your torso, just above the belly button.

Wrap the tape snugly around your natural waist. Breathe normally — don't hold your breath.
Hip 86 – 114 cm

The fullest part of your hips and seat — approx. 20 cm below the waist.

Stand feet together. Keep the tape level all the way around.
Thigh

Upper thigh circumference, just below the hip joint.

Measure around the fullest part of the thigh, close to the hip.

Fit Guide

How each style fits
Wide Leg

Wide & Relaxed

Generous through the hip and thigh, with a wide straight leg. True to size — size up for extra flow.

Balloon

Relaxed Balloon

High-waisted with a roomy balloon silhouette that tapers slightly at the ankle. Size down if in between.

High Waist

Fitted through the hip with a high waist. Stretchy fabric allows for comfort. True to size.

Sizing
Tips
  • Always measure yourself — don't rely on clothing from other brands as sizes vary.
  • Our denim has minimal stretch, so measure carefully and don't round down.
  • If your waist and hip measurements fall in different sizes, size up to accommodate the hips.
  • Still unsure? Contact us on WhatsApp — we'll help you pick the right size.
